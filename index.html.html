Each pick is random, then redrawn if it falls into a pattern lots of people bet on: multiples of one number, three in a row, all birthday numbers (1–31), the same last digit, or numbers bunched together.

On Oct 1, 2022, Grand Lotto 6/55 drew 9-18-27-36-45-54. 433 people had bet it, so ₱236 million became about ₱545,000 each.

Draw days follow PCSO’s usual schedule and can change on holidays. Play only what you can afford to lose.
